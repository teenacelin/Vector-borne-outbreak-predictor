# VectorPredict Core Business Brief

## 1. Which institution is this for?
This platform is developed for Brazilian public health authorities and epidemiological teams, facilitated through the Living Stones Foundation - Applied Data & Digital Innovation Lab (LATAM).

## 2. Who are the users?
The primary users are regional health officials, epidemiologists, and mosquito-control deployment teams who need to allocate limited public health resources (such as targeted spraying or health warnings).

## 3. What problem do they want solved?
They need to transition from a reactive response to a proactive early-warning system. By predicting malaria outbreaks weeks in advance based on climate triggers (temperature and rainfall), officials can deploy interventions before cases overwhelm local health infrastructure.

## 4. What the dataset actually offers
- **Disease Data:** SIVEP-Malaria cases covering November 2024 to December 2025. Note: The dataset only contains Malaria cases, not Dengue.
- **Climate Data:** CHIRPS rainfall and ERA5 temperature data for the same time period.
- **Geography:** GADM Level 2 boundaries for Brazilian municipalities.
- **Coverage:** 125 distinct municipalities.

## 5. Which municipalities, for which health authority?
The dataset covers 125 municipalities across Brazil. Proper identification requires matching by IBGE Code (CC_2 in GADM) or a combination of State and Municipality names, ensuring that disease data accurately maps to the correct local health authority's jurisdiction (e.g., distinguishing Alto Alegre, Roraima from Alto Alegre, São Paulo).
